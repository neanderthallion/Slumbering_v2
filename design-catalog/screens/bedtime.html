Getting enough sleep (8–10 hours a day) can help you feel more focused, energized, and ready for the day.

Purpose

The dedicated analytics page for bedtime consistency — week/month series for bedtime, wake time, and duration, each with a regularity proxy, plus a single highest-priority personalized tip.

Data & state

  • Series are built from recordsStore (features/bedtime/logic/series.ts), gated the same as everywhere else — a night only counts once checkInCompletedAt is set and sleepConfirmation is non-null.
  • Bedtimes wrap past midnight — lib/dateMath.ts's normalizeBedtimeMinutes/normalizedMinutesToTime shift any pre-noon clock time into "the next day" before averaging/variance, so a 12:30 AM bedtime doesn't skew the average toward noon.
  • Regularity (features/bedtime/logic/regularity.ts) is a population-SD proxy, bucketed: High (≤30 min) / Moderate (30–60 min) / Irregular (60–90 min) / Severely Irregular (≥90 min). There's no true Sleep Regularity Index without continuous epoch data.
  • The recommendation (recommendations.ts) is a single date-seeded tip chosen by priority: short average duration (<8h) > irregular bedtime/wake timing > late average bedtime vs. target > late average wake time vs. target > general tips fallback. Never more than one shown at a time.

Conditional states

Week/Month toggle re-scopes all three series and the regularity/recommendation calculations together — switching period never mixes data from the other window.

User actions & navigation

  • Back row → Home
  • Reached by tapping the Home Bedtime Schedule panel outside its two inline time fields (which edit bedtime/wake in place instead)

Components used

Card, Tabs, Line chart, Bar chart.
