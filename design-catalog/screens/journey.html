Purpose

The full picture: lifetime success rate, the 7 fixed badges, and every logged night/milestone in one scrollable, reverse-chronological timeline.

Data & state

  • All-time progress ring (% of checked-in nights fully achieved) + "N night(s) logged" + fully-successful count + longest streak.
  • 7 fixed BADGE_DEFINITIONS (badges.ts) always render in a horizontally scrolling row; unearned ones render dim/locked.
  • History merges night records with milestone entries (e.g. "First night on Slumbering.") into one reverse-chronological list. Entries added via Add Past Record are labeled "Added later." Empty-state text when there are no entries yet.

The 7 badges

BadgeTrigger
First StepCompleted your first check-in ever.
Full Week7 calendar-consecutive days with at least one goal achieved each night (not full achievement).
One MonthCumulative, non-consecutive count of 30 checked-in nights all-time.
Welcome BackReturned and checked in after a ≥5-day gap — a once-ever celebration, deliberately a different threshold from the 3+ day "returning" companion-message tier.
Go GetterReaches a fully-achieved-nights milestone.
Full NightAccumulates (non-consecutively) 10 nights whose derived sleep duration is ≥7 hours.
Steady RhythmA 14 calendar-consecutive-night run whose bedtimes each fall within ±30 min of that window's own average.

There is no "Early Riser" badge — all 7 names above are the current, final roster.

User actions & navigation

  • Back row → Home
  • Floating action button (calendar icon) → Add Past Record (native modal)

Components used

Card, Progress ring, Badge medallion, timeline rail, FAB.
