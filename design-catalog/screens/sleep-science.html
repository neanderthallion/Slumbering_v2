Sleep science references

1. The Core Premise: Psychology and Sleep Science

Modern society faces a paradox: millions of young adults suffer from insufficient or inconsistent sleep despite widespread knowledge of its importance. The Slumbering app is built on a specific scientific premise: behavior change in sleep is most sustainable when driven by user autonomy, micro-commitments, and psychological safety, rather than surveillance and algorithmic control.

The "Quantified Self" Anxiety

Detailed tracking can induce orthosomnia — an unhealthy preoccupation with achieving "perfect" sleep metrics. High anxiety directly stimulates hyperarousal, worsening insomnia.

The Fatigue of Surveillance

Requiring users to wear devices, log complex variables, or adhere to rigid schedules creates cognitive fatigue. Users abandon apps that feel like a second job.

Shame and the Extinction Burst

Punitive metrics or broken-streak shaming trigger psychological resistance rather than renewed motivation.
