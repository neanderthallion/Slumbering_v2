==================================================
 SLUMBERING — USER MANUAL
==================================================

Welcome to Slumbering, a friendly companion app that helps you build
better sleep habits, one small goal at a time. No pressure, no shame —
just gentle encouragement and a clear picture of your progress.

This guide covers everything you'll see and do in the app.


--------------------------------------------------
1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------

The first time you open Slumbering, you'll be asked to:

  - Tell us what to call you (optional — you can skip this or change
    it later in Settings).
  - Choose Light or Dark theme.

That's it — tap "Get started" and you're in.


--------------------------------------------------
2. HOME SCREEN
--------------------------------------------------

Home is where you land every time you open the app. It shows:

  - A greeting with your name and a short encouraging message.
  - Your main action for right now (see below).
  - A quick look at your last 7 nights.
  - Your current bedtime and wake-up schedule.

What you'll see in the middle of the screen changes depending on
where you are in your day:

  - No goals picked yet tonight -> a button to pick your goals.
  - Goals already picked -> a summary of tonight's goals, with an
    "Edit" link if you want to change them.
  - Morning, ready to check in -> a button asking how last night went.
  - Everything done for today -> a simple "come back tomorrow" note.

Tap the gear icon (top right) any time to open Settings.


--------------------------------------------------
3. SETTING TONIGHT'S GOALS
--------------------------------------------------

Tap "Pick your goals for tonight" from Home to choose what you want
to focus on. A couple of tips:

  - Goals are grouped into three categories: Bedtime, Unwind, and
    Relaxation. Tap a category to see its suggestions.
  - Pick as many as you like — but sticking to 1–2 goals is usually
    easier to follow through on.
  - Tap the pencil icon next to any suggested goal to rename it to
    fit your own routine.
  - Don't see what you want? Type your own goal into "Add your own…"
    and tap Add.
  - If you pick a very late bedtime, the app will gently check that
    you meant to — you can continue anyway or change the goal.

When you're happy with your choices, tap "Confirm goals". You'll see
a final recap screen — tap Confirm again to lock them in for the
night.


--------------------------------------------------
4. CHECKING IN
--------------------------------------------------

The next time you open the app, Home will ask how last night went.

  - First you'll be asked a simple yes/no question: did you keep to
    your planned bedtime and wake time?
      - Answer "Yes" and you're done with the timing question —
        no extra typing needed.
      - Answer "No" and you can enter what time you actually went to
        bed and woke up, if you'd like.
  - Next, mark each of last night's goals as achieved or not.
  - Tap Confirm to save your check-in.

If a check-in unlocks a new badge, you'll see a small celebration
before returning Home. Missing a goal is never treated as a failure —
you'll never see language like "streak broken" in this app. Every day
you show up is a win.


--------------------------------------------------
5. YOUR JOURNEY
--------------------------------------------------

Tap "See all" on the Journey preview (or find it from Home) to see
the full picture:

  - An all-time success ring showing the percentage of nights you've
    fully achieved your goals, plus your total nights logged and your
    longest streak.
  - Your badges — seven of them in total, earned by hitting different
    milestones (first check-in, consistent weeks, cumulative nights,
    returning after a break, and more). Locked badges appear dimmed
    until you earn them.
  - A full history of every night you've logged, most recent first,
    including notes for nights you added later and milestones like
    your very first night on Slumbering.

Tap the calendar button in the corner to add a night you forgot to
log (see section 6).


--------------------------------------------------
6. ADDING A PAST RECORD
--------------------------------------------------

Forgot to log a night? No problem. From the Journey screen, tap the
floating calendar button to open "Add a past record":

  - Choose the date.
  - Pick the goals that applied that night.
  - Mark which ones you achieved.
  - Optionally, add the actual bedtime and wake-up time if you
    remember them.
  - Tap Save.

These entries are clearly marked "added later" in your Journey
timeline, so your history stays honest and easy to follow.


--------------------------------------------------
7. BEDTIME SCHEDULE & STATS
--------------------------------------------------

Your current bedtime and wake-up time are always shown on Home. Tap
either time to change it right there.

Tap the Bedtime Schedule panel itself to open a more detailed view
with:

  - Week or Month view (switch between them at the top).
  - A chart of your bedtime over time, compared to your target, with
    a regularity rating (how consistent you've been).
  - The same chart for your wake-up time.
  - A chart of how much sleep you've been getting each night.
  - One personalized tip based on whatever matters most right now
    (for example, getting more total sleep, or keeping a steadier
    bedtime).


--------------------------------------------------
8. SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------

Open Settings from the gear icon on Home. Everything is on one
scrolling screen:

  PROFILE
    Change the name Slumbering uses to greet you.

  REMINDERS
    Three optional daily reminders, each with its own on/off switch:
      - Set goals reminder — nudges you to pick tonight's goals.
      - Check-in reminder — nudges you to check in the next morning.
      - Unwind reminder — reminds you shortly before your bedtime.
    Turning a reminder on will ask for notification permission the
    first time.

  APPEARANCE
    - Theme: Light or Dark.
    - Text size: Small, Medium, or Large.
    Both changes apply instantly across the whole app.

  BACKUP & RESTORE
    - Export backup: saves all your goals, history, and settings to a
      file you can keep somewhere safe.
    - Import backup: restores everything from a previously exported
      file. This replaces your current data, so you'll be asked to
      confirm before it happens.

  APP INFORMATION
    - App version.
    - Sleep science references — the research behind how the app is
      designed.
    - Terms of service, Contact us, and Feedback links.


--------------------------------------------------
9. A FEW THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND
--------------------------------------------------

  - Small goals count. You don't need a perfect night to make
    progress — partial credit still moves you forward.
  - Missing a night, or several, is never punished. Come back
    whenever you're ready and pick up where you left off.
  - Your data stays on your device unless you export a backup
    yourself — there's no account or cloud sync.
  - Everything in this app is meant to be quick. If a screen is
    asking you to fill in a lot of detail, you can usually skip it.

Sleep well.
